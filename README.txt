SAT Practice Lab — All Eight Domains

1. Extract the entire ZIP folder.
2. Open the sat_practice_lab folder.
3. Double-click index.html in Chrome or Edge.
4. Choose a section, then choose a domain. The domain dropdown becomes active after selecting Mathematics or Reading & Writing.
5. Click Start [Domain] Test.

IMPORTANT: Keep index.html, questions.js, and the assets folder together. Do not open index.html from inside the ZIP preview.

If your browser blocks local JavaScript, use the included start_local_server.bat and then open the address it displays (http://localhost:8000).
